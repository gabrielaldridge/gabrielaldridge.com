Squominoes

General Rules:

  1. Each player gets 6 cards.
  2. The first player places a card on the table.
  3. Players take turns placing a card that connects to the last card played.
  4. If you connect to more than one card, you may play an extra card for each new connection.
  5. After each turn, draw a card from the deck.
  6. If you can't play a card, draw one and skip your turn.
  7. The first player to get rid of all their cards wins.
  8. If no one can play, the player with the fewest cards wins.
